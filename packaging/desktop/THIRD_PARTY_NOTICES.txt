VTS desktop includes Python, pywebview, PyObjC (macOS) or pythonnet/.NET interop
(Windows), and the Python distributions listed in python-distributions.json.
Their license texts are provided in licenses/ and distribution metadata.
PyInstaller's bootloader exception permits distribution of frozen applications.

FFmpeg 9.0.2 is built from the exact sources in binaries.json, using the included
build_ffmpeg.sh. No GPL or nonfree components are enabled. FFmpeg is LGPL 2.1+
(with relevant files under their original compatible licenses), LAME 3.100 is
LGPL 2.0+, and zlib 1.3.2 uses the zlib license. See the license texts and LAME's
static-linking notice in licenses/. FFmpeg is a separate executable and can be
replaced with a compatible build by the user. The source download URLs, SHA256
hashes, compiler information and complete build recipe are included with VTS.
Native TLS uses Secure Transport on macOS and Schannel on Windows.

VTS is MIT licensed. WebView2 (Windows) is supplied separately by Microsoft;
WKWebView (macOS) is supplied by the operating system.
